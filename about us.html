About WhoopeeWebs

We're a team of passionate students who believe that choosing the right path in tech shouldn't be a guessing game. We built WhoopeeWebs to change that.

Our Mission

Time flies when ideas take off.

The internet has connected the world to a whole new level of information sharing. Yet, when a student starts their journey in computer science, their knowledge about the field is often limited. They go with the crowd, pick mainstream options, and often give up.

WhoopeeWebs is our attempt at killing the cliché — connecting students with alumni, professionals, and curated resources so they can discover what truly excites them.

  • Find the path that fits you, not the crowd's expectations.
  • Connect with alumni and professionals on GitHub & LinkedIn.
  • Access curated resources across every major tech domain.
  • Explore chances across domains: AI, Data, Security, Web, and more.
